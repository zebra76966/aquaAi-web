2Description of Service

2.1Aqua AI is an AI-powered aquatic intelligence platform. The Aqua AI app (“Users App”) provides:

  • AI-driven fish species identification via image, video, and live video analysis
  • Disease detection through computer vision and symptom analysis
  • Water parameter analysis via test strip photo upload and manual entry (standard and saltwater parameters including pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, oxygen, temperature, magnesium, alkalinity, calcium, and phosphates)
  • AI Water Intelligence that interprets test results into practical guidance
  • Dynamic species compatibility assessment with local availability data
  • Comparative species tracking for monitoring fish growth over time
  • AI-generated care plans, maintenance schedules, and custom task creation
  • Tank health scoring with a visible health scale
  • Product recommendations with dosage calculations, tracking, and reorder alerts
  • Equipment management with detailed tracking per habitat
  • AquaBot — an AI assistant for text and voice queries (voice where available on your device)
  • A peer-to-peer marketplace for aquatic goods
  • Consultant discovery, booking, and payment across three service categories (Consultancy, Pond, Tank) with over 20 service types
  • Breeder directory and in-app purchasing of breeder stock
  • Community Showcase, where users can choose to display their tanks, receive likes, and message other users

2.2The Aqua Providers app enables verified professional service providers to manage their operations:

  • Breeders: species stock management with auto-stock reduction, size variations, duplicate listings, marketplace integration, analytics, Trust Intelligence, Research tools, and the Provider AI Business Assistant
  • Consultants: service listings, availability calendar, booking management, earnings tracking, and performance analytics

2.3All AI outputs are probabilistic and advisory. AI-generated insights do not constitute professional veterinary, scientific, or medical advice. Consult a qualified aquatic specialist for critical health concerns.

6Consultant Booking and Service Terms

6.1The Platform lets users find, book, and pay for professional aquatic consultancy services in three categories: Consultancy (coaching, education, one-off advice, testing, urgent support, water quality), Pond (construction, equipment, filtration, fish health, maintenance, seasonal, water quality), and Tank (design, breakdown, disease, equipment, maintenance, setup, stocking, water assessments). Bookings are available on all tiers, including Free.

6.2How bookings are paid. Aqua AI’s booking fee is 10% of the service price and is taken from it, not added to it. For example, for a £100 service you pay £10 in the app to confirm the booking, and pay the remaining £90 to the consultant directly (for example in cash or by bank transfer, as agreed with the consultant). The booking fee can be paid by card, with platform credit, or both. Platform credit can only be used for the booking fee, so no more than 10% of the service price can be paid with credit on any booking.

6.3Booking messages between users and consultants are limited to active bookings. A booking thread opens when the booking is confirmed and closes on completion or cancellation.

6.4Consultants have 72 hours to accept or decline a booking request. Requests with no response expire automatically.

6.5Cancellation Policy

  • Cancellation by the user with 48 hours’ notice or more: the booking fee is not refunded, whether paid by card or with credit.
  • Cancellation by the user with less than 48 hours’ notice, or the user does not attend: no refund.
  • Cancellation by the consultant, or the consultant does not attend: the booking fee is refunded, to your card or as credit if you paid with credit (see 6.7). A consultant no-show is also recorded against the consultant’s trust score. Instead of cancelling, the consultant may offer an alternative time, which applies only if you agree.
  • Any amount you paid directly to the consultant is a matter between you and the consultant.
  • Cancelling with less than 48 hours’ notice is also subject to trust score accountability (L1–L4 escalation based on pattern), for whichever party cancels. Users are encouraged to reschedule rather than cancel.

6.6Rescheduling. Rescheduling only updates the booking record. No payment is processed. Both parties may reschedule by mutual agreement.

6.7Consultant Cancellation and Refunds. The booking fee is refunded to the original payment method via Stripe, or returned as credit if paid with credit. Aqua AI absorbs the processing fee. Consultant accountability is handled through trust scores, not financial penalties.

10Trust Score and Badge System

10.1Trust scores are calculated from verified platform activity: water testing, maintenance completion, marketplace conduct, booking behaviour, community participation, and general engagement. Scores are not self-declared.

10.2Badges (for example, Responsible Keeper, Trusted Trader, Verified Consultant) are earned through evidence-based criteria with deterministic thresholds. Badge points contribute to trust tiers: Bronze (0–39), Silver (40–79), Gold (80–119), Platinum (120+).

10.3Incidents are classified L1 (-5 points) to L4 (-30 to -40 points). L1–L3 decay over 180–365 days. L4 may persist permanently.

10.4Trust scores are recalculated automatically. Trust score and badge changes are recorded in a tamper-evident (hash-chained) audit log.

10.5Trust score affects user ranking, badge progress, marketplace visibility, and consultant/breeder search priority. Badges and trust scores may decay or be revoked if standards are not maintained.
