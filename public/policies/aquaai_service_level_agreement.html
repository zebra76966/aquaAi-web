1Overview

1.1This Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) sets out the service commitments AQUAAI LTD, trading as Aqua AI, makes to users and providers on platform availability, performance, safety, and support.

1.2It applies to the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers apps and the website at www.aquaai.uk. The figures below are service targets.

2Platform Availability

2.1We target 99.5% monthly uptime for core platform services, excluding scheduled maintenance.

2.2Core platform services are user authentication, tank management, AI scanning and identification, marketplace browsing and listing, consultant booking, breeder directory and in-app breeder commerce, community features, and push notifications.

2.3Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance where possible, and is carried out during low-usage hours (typically 02:00–06:00 UK time).

3AI Service Performance

3.1AI response time targets under normal load:

  • Species identification (image): within 6 seconds
  • Species identification (video): within 10 seconds for 10-second clips
  • Disease detection: within 8 seconds
  • Water parameter analysis: within 4 seconds
  • AquaBot: within 5 seconds per query
  • Care plan generation: within 10 seconds

3.2These targets exclude periods of exceptional demand, third-party outages, and force majeure.

4Payments and Payouts

4.1Website subscriptions and in-app bookings and purchases are processed through Stripe. App Store subscriptions are processed by Apple, and their billing and refunds are subject to Apple’s own terms and timescales.

4.2Refunds we issue (see our Refund Policy) are processed automatically where the policy says so, and usually arrive within 5–10 business days. Amounts paid with platform credit are returned to your credit balance.

4.3Breeder commerce: funds for collection orders are released automatically when the buyer’s collection code is entered at handover; funds for delivery orders are held for 7 days and then released automatically. Stripe Connect typically pays out to the breeder’s bank 2–3 business days after release.

4.4Payment processing availability depends on Stripe and Apple and is outside our direct control.

5Data Integrity

5.1Platform data is backed up daily so that it can be restored after a failure.

5.2Trust scores are recalculated automatically from verified platform activity.

5.3Trust score and badge changes are recorded in a hash-chained audit log, so that any later alteration can be detected.

6Safety and Moderation

6.1Reports of objectionable content or abusive users are acted on within 24 hours. Content that breaches our Terms is removed, and the user who posted it is removed from the Platform.

6.2When you block a user, their content is removed from your view immediately.

7Support

7.1Support is available by email at support@aquaai.uk.

7.2Initial response targets:

  • Critical (platform outage, data loss, security incident): within 4 hours
  • High (payment issues, booking failures, account access): within 24 hours
  • Standard (feature questions, feedback, general enquiries): within 48 hours
  • Low (suggestions, non-urgent feedback): within 5 business days

8Incident Management

8.1Platform incidents are handled as follows:

  • Detection: incidents are identified by our team or reported by users
  • Assessment: severity is classified within 30 minutes of detection
  • Communication: users are notified of incidents that affect service availability
  • Resolution: root cause is identified and fixed
  • Review: each significant incident is reviewed and preventative action recorded

8.2Personal data breaches are also handled under our Data Processing Notice, including notification to the ICO within 72 hours where required.

9Exclusions

9.1This SLA does not cover planned maintenance announced in advance; outages caused by third-party providers (including Stripe, Apple, Google, AWS, Supabase, and OpenAI); issues caused by your device, configuration, or connection; force majeure; or beta and preview features.

10If We Fall Short

10.1If core platform availability falls below 99.5% in a calendar month, contact support@aquaai.uk with details of the disruption. We will review it and tell you what we can offer.

10.2Nothing in this SLA affects your statutory rights as a consumer.

11Changes

We may update this SLA from time to time. Changes are communicated through the Platform.
