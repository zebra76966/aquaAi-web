1Scope

1.1These terms apply to subscriptions purchased at www.aquaai.uk. They supplement our Terms and Conditions and form part of them for anyone who takes up an offer below.

2Standard Prices

  • Premium: £14.99/month or £144.99/year
  • Pro: £24.99/month or £239.99/year
  • Breeder: £24.99/month or £239.99/year

Consultants pay no subscription. Aqua AI’s booking fee is 10% of the consultant’s service price, paid by the user in the app; the user pays the remaining 90% to the consultant directly. Breeders pay a 7% platform commission on in-app sales.

Optional breeder certification: Aqua Certified £250/year; Professional £699/year; Master Breeder £1,499/year.

3Launch Promotional Pricing

3.1Promotional pricing is available to the first 1,000 qualifying Premium and Pro subscribers and the first 100 qualifying breeders who subscribe at www.aquaai.uk.

  • Premium: £11.99/month or £114.99/year
  • Pro: £19.99/month or £189.99/year
  • Breeder (founding rate): £19.99/month or £189.99/year

3.2Premium and Pro subscribers qualify on their first successful payment.

3.3Breeders qualify as set out in Section 4.

4Breeder Free Trial and Founding Places

4.1New breeders receive a 60-day free trial, starting on the date their provider account is approved.

4.2A valid payment method is needed to start the trial. Nothing is charged during it. The first payment is taken when the trial ends.

4.3While founding places remain, the subscription is at the founding rate in Section 3.1. The founding place is secured when the trial starts with a valid payment method.

4.4The founding place is forfeited if the trial is cancelled before the first payment, or if the first payment fails and is not recovered. A forfeited place returns to the pool. A breeder who forfeits a founding place pays standard pricing on return.

4.5The 7% sales commission applies from the first day, including during the trial.

5Keeping Your Promotional Price (Grandfathering)

5.1You keep your promotional price for as long as your paid subscription continues without a break.

5.2If you cancel a paid subscription, a 30-day grace period applies. Re-subscribing within 30 days restores your promotional price.

5.3If the grace period ends without re-subscription, the promotional entitlement is permanently lost and standard pricing applies on return.

5.4No refunds are given for a forfeited promotional entitlement or founding place. Nothing is charged during the breeder trial, so there is nothing to refund if you cancel during it.
