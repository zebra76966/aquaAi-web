1Introduction

1.1This Provider Policy governs the relationship between AQUAAI LTD, trading as Aqua AI, and the professional providers who operate through the Aqua Providers app. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions, which also apply to you.

1.2Providers are Consultants and Breeders. You can hold both roles in the same account.

1.3You must be 18 or over, and you must accept this policy before your provider account is activated.

2Registration and Approval

2.1You apply at www.aquaai.uk, including when the application is opened from the Aqua Providers app. Your application includes business or personal details, service or species information, qualifications or experience, and social or web presence.

2.2Head office reviews applications, usually within 24–48 hours. Once approved, sign in to the Aqua Providers app to access your dashboard.

2.3You can apply for the other role at any time.

3Fees and Commission

3.1Consultants pay no subscription. Aqua AI’s booking fee is 10% of your service price, taken from it and paid by the user in the app (by card or platform credit) to confirm the booking. The user pays the remaining 90% to you directly, in cash or by bank transfer as you agree with them. Aqua AI does not collect or hold your 90%.

3.2Breeders pay a subscription and a 7% platform commission on in-app sales, deducted at payout. The commission includes payment processing fees, and you keep 93% of each sale. Subscription fees are shown when you subscribe.

3.3Breeder certification (optional): Aqua Certified, Professional, and Master Breeder tiers are evidence-based and earned through verified activity, not self-declared. Certification fees are shown when you apply.

3.4Platform credit. Users can use referral platform credit only for Aqua AI’s booking fee on consultant bookings, and towards breeder purchases. When credit is used on a breeder purchase, the breeder still receives their full 93%, as though the user had paid in full by card.

4Consultant Obligations

4.1You must:

  • keep service listings, pricing, and availability accurate
  • respond to booking requests within 72 hours
  • honour confirmed bookings and provide services as described
  • maintain professional standards in all interactions
  • comply with applicable laws and professional standards
  • keep communication about a booking in the booking’s messaging thread

4.2Cancellation and Rescheduling

  • Reschedule rather than cancel. The cancel button offers rescheduling first.
  • If you cannot keep a booking, you may offer the user an alternative time. It applies only if the user agrees.
  • If you cancel or do not attend, the user’s booking fee is refunded. Aqua AI absorbs the Stripe fee. Not attending is also recorded against your trust score.
  • Any payment the user has made to you directly is a matter between you and the user.
  • Cancelling with less than 48 hours’ notice is recorded as a trust score incident (L1–L4 escalation based on pattern). Cancellations with more notice are counted, with an alert at every third occurrence.

4.3Availability. Set your weekly availability (default: weekdays) and block time when you are unavailable.

5Breeder Obligations

5.1You must:

  • keep species stock levels, pricing, and availability accurate
  • describe species health, lineage, and provenance truthfully
  • comply with UK law on the sale and transport of live aquatic animals
  • respond to enquiries promptly
  • update stock when sold or unavailable (stock reduces automatically on sale)
  • never list protected, endangered, or illegally sourced species
  • enter health and mortality data accurately

5.2Species and Stock Management. Manage species by type (Freshwater, Saltwater, Pond fish, Inverts). Increase, reduce, mark sold out, or restock. Size variations and listing duplication are supported. When stock reaches zero, the listing can be removed while the species record is kept for restocking.

5.3Listings. List species directly from your inventory. Each listing needs at least one image. Listings do not expire automatically; remove them when stock is unavailable.

6Breeder Commerce

6.1Stripe Connect. To sell in the app you need a valid Stripe Connect account. You submit identity and bank details directly to Stripe; Aqua AI does not store them.

6.2Orders and payment. Buyers order and pay in the app. Funds are released automatically to your Stripe account, less the 7% commission: for collection orders, as soon as you enter the buyer’s collection code at handover; for delivery orders, after a 7-day hold.

6.3Fulfilment. Buyers choose collection or delivery, using the collection and delivery settings you configure. You must make stock available for collection, or dispatch it, within 7 days of the order. Failure to fulfil may lead to an automatic refund to the buyer and a trust score penalty. If a buyer does not collect within 7 days, mark the order as not collected: the buyer is refunded in full, your stock is restored, and your trust score is not affected.

6.4Licence for delivery. To offer delivery you must hold a current licence for selling animals as pets, issued by your local council, and upload it in the Providers app for approval. Aqua AI checks it against the council’s public register before approving it. Delivery is available only while an approved licence is in date: if it expires or an approval is withdrawn, delivery pauses until a current licence is approved. Collection does not need a licence. You are responsible for holding any licence the law requires for your activity, whether or not you offer delivery.

6.5Buyer contact details. Use the buyer details you receive only to fulfil that order.

6.6Prohibited. Directing buyers to pay off-platform, inflating delivery charges to avoid commission, or listing stock that is not genuinely available.

7Provider Tools

7.1Analytics: read-only dashboards covering sales, stock, species, ratings, health, mortality, response times, trust score, tier comparison, inventory, seasonal forecasts, and rating distribution, with AI-generated insights.

7.2Trust Intelligence: AI-powered performance review covering scores, trajectory, benchmarks, rank, weekly digest, risk breakdown, retention, demand and performance forecasting, and badge progress.

7.3Provider AI Business Assistant: an AI assistant (text and voice, voice where available on your device) for prompts, reports, business guidance, profile improvement, growth support, and stock and sales planning, personalised to your provider profile.

7.4Research Tool: market research, competitor review, recommended products and species, and intelligence reports.

7.5All AI output is advisory and is not financial advice.

8Trust Score and Badges

8.1Providers use the same trust score system as users (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum). Provider badges include Verified Consultant, Specialist, Fast Responder, Verified Breeder, Healthy Stock Signal, Certified Lineage, and Consistent Performer.

8.2Badges are awarded on verifiable metrics such as booking completion, response times, review authenticity, and stock health signals. Trust scores are visible to users and affect discovery, ranking, and priority.

9Provider Conduct

9.1You must not solicit off-platform transactions, misrepresent qualifications or credentials, share client data outside service delivery, engage in anti-competitive behaviour, or engage in animal cruelty or neglect.

9.2Our zero-tolerance policy on objectionable content and abusive behaviour (Terms Section 12 and the Acceptable Use Policy) applies to everything you post or send, including listings, profiles, and messages.

10Ratings and Reviews

10.1Users can rate and review you. Providers cannot currently reply to reviews in the app; this may be added later.

11Suspension, Termination, and Closure

11.1L4 breaches, and breaches of our zero-tolerance policy, result in immediate removal. On removal, we will cancel and refund your open bookings and orders, deactivate your listings, and stop subscription charges.

11.2To close your provider account, first fulfil or cancel pending bookings and orders, deactivate your listings, and tell active clients. You can then delete your account in the app or at www.aquaai.uk/profile/delete/.
