1. Parties and Scope

1.1. This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Terms and Conditions between Humara Ltd, trading as AQUA AI® ("Data Controller" / "we"), and the user of the Platform ("Data Subject" / "you").

1.2. This DPA also governs the relationship between AQUA AI® as Data Controller and our third-party service providers acting as Data Processors on our behalf.

1.3. This DPA is governed by the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Definitions

2.1. "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, as defined in Article 4(1) of the UK GDPR.

2.2. "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, as defined in Article 4(2) of the UK GDPR.

2.3. "Data Processor" means a third party who processes personal data on behalf of the Data Controller.

2.4. "Platform" means the AQUA AI® and Aqua Providers mobile applications and the website at www.aquaai.uk.

3. Data Controller Obligations

3.1. AQUA AI®, as Data Controller, is responsible for:

  • Determining the purposes and means of processing personal data
  • Ensuring a lawful basis exists for all processing activities
  • Maintaining records of processing activities under Article 30 of the UK GDPR
  • Implementing appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data
  • Notifying the ICO and affected individuals of personal data breaches within 72 hours where required

4. Categories of Data Processed

  • Identity data: name, email address, profile information
  • Authentication data: login credentials, third-party OAuth tokens
  • Aquarium data: tank configurations, species, water parameters, equipment, care schedules
  • AI interaction data: queries, images, video, AI-generated insights
  • Transactional data: booking records, payment references, subscription status
  • Marketplace data: listings, bids, purchase requests, seller profiles
  • Trust data: trust scores, badge history, incident records
  • Communication data: booking-scoped messages between users and consultants
  • Device and usage data: device identifiers, app usage patterns, performance metrics
  • Location data: approximate and precise location (where consent is provided)

6. Data Security Measures

6.1. AQUA AI® implements the following technical and organisational measures:

  • EncryptionTLS/HTTPS for all data in transit; AES-256 encryption at rest
  • AuthenticationJWT-based with 5-minute access tokens and 7-day refresh rotation
  • Access controlBackend-enforced ACL as primary layer; Supabase Row Level Security as secondary
  • Audit loggingImmutable logs on all privileged operations
  • MFAMandatory for all operational and administrative accounts
  • Environment variablesAll secrets and API keys managed via environment variables, never hardcoded
  • Rate limiting100 requests per hour per user via Redis
  • Password securityPBKDF2 hashing for stored credentials

7. International Transfers

7.1. Where personal data is transferred outside the United Kingdom, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with Chapter V of the UK GDPR, including: UK Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions by the UK Government, or binding corporate rules where applicable.

8. Data Subject Rights

8.1. AQUA AI® facilitates the exercise of data subject rights as set out in Chapter III of the UK GDPR. Sub-processors are contractually required to assist in fulfilling these requests. Requests are processed within 30 days.

9. Data Breach Notification

9.1. In the event of a personal data breach, AQUA AI® will:

  • Assess the breach within 24 hours of discovery
  • Notify the ICO within 72 hours where the breach is likely to result in a risk to individuals' rights
  • Notify affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk
  • Document the breach, its effects, and remedial actions taken

10. Data Retention and Deletion

10.1. Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, as detailed in our Privacy Policy. Upon account deletion or expiry of retention periods, personal data is securely deleted or anonymised.

11. Audit Rights

11.1. AQUA AI® maintains the right to audit sub-processors' compliance with data processing obligations. Users may request information about our data processing practices by contacting info@aquaai.uk.
