1Introduction

1.1This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) sets out the rules for using the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers mobile applications and the website at www.aquaai.uk (together, the “Platform”).

1.2This AUP forms part of our Terms and Conditions. By using the Platform, you agree to comply with it.

1.3Breaching this AUP may lead to content removal, trust score penalties (L1–L4), feature restrictions, account suspension, or permanent removal from the Platform.

2Zero Tolerance for Objectionable Content and Abusive Users

2.1Aqua AI has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. This applies to everything users post, upload, or send on the Platform: Community Showcase posts, images, and comments; marketplace listings; profiles; reviews; and all messages (Showcase, marketplace, and booking).

2.2You must not post, upload, or send content that:

  • is sexually explicit or pornographic
  • is hateful, or discriminates against anyone on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, or any other protected characteristic
  • harasses, bullies, intimidates, or abuses any person
  • threatens or promotes violence
  • is defamatory, or invades anyone’s privacy
  • depicts, promotes, or encourages animal cruelty or neglect
  • promotes illegal activity, including the illegal trade in protected species
  • is spam, a scam, or misleading
  • infringes anyone’s intellectual property or other rights

2.3We use automated filtering and human review to detect and prevent objectionable content from being posted.

2.4Content that breaches this section is removed, and the user responsible is removed from the Platform.

3General Conduct

3.1You must use the Platform lawfully, honestly, and in good faith.

3.2You must treat all users, providers, and platform staff with respect and professionalism.

3.3You must provide accurate information in your profile, listings, bookings, and all platform interactions.

4Prohibited Activities

4.1You must not:

  • Use the Platform for any unlawful purpose or in breach of any applicable law or regulation
  • Submit false, misleading, or fraudulent information, including fabricated reviews, inflated credentials, or inaccurate listing descriptions
  • Harass, abuse, threaten, defame, or discriminate against any user, provider, or staff member
  • Impersonate any person or entity, or misrepresent your identity, qualifications, or affiliation
  • Create multiple accounts to get around restrictions or blocks, manipulate trust scores, or abuse the referral programme
  • Contact or follow a user who has blocked you, including through another account
  • List or sell prohibited, illegal, stolen, counterfeit, or dangerous goods
  • List or sell protected, endangered, or illegally sourced species
  • Arrange off-platform transactions designed to get around platform commissions, trust systems, or dispute resolution
  • Circumvent the Stripe payment integration for breeder commerce, including directing buyers to pay off-platform or using alternative payment methods to avoid the platform commission
  • Manipulate stock listings, pricing, or delivery charges to misrepresent the true cost of goods or to get around the commission structure
  • Interfere with, disrupt, or compromise the security, integrity, or performance of the Platform
  • Use automated tools, bots, scrapers, or crawlers to access the Platform
  • Reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or extract source code from the Platform
  • Collect, harvest, or store other users’ personal data without their explicit consent
  • Spam other users through marketplace, booking, Showcase, or messaging features
  • Manipulate trust scores, badges, likes, or reviews through coordinated or fraudulent activity
  • Make false or malicious reports

5Marketplace Rules

5.1All marketplace listings must be accurate, complete, and honestly described.

5.2Sellers must honour accepted offers and purchase requests. Repeated failure to complete accepted transactions will lead to trust score penalties.

5.3Listings must comply with UK law on the sale of live animals, including species-specific regulations and welfare requirements.

5.4Prices must be in British Pounds Sterling and reflect the true transaction value.

5.5Prohibited marketplace items include protected or endangered species, illegally caught or imported specimens, controlled substances, items that put animal welfare at risk, and any goods prohibited by UK law.

6Booking and Consultation Rules

6.1Users must give accurate information when requesting bookings and must attend or cancel within the required notice periods.

6.2Consultants must provide services as described, maintain professional standards, and honour confirmed bookings.

6.3Communication between users and consultants about a booking must take place in the booking’s messaging thread. Moving communication off-platform to avoid platform oversight breaches this AUP.

7Community Showcase Conduct

7.1Keep the Community Showcase respectful and constructive.

7.2Harassment, spam, and using Showcase messaging to arrange off-platform transactions are prohibited. Sales must go through marketplace listings or breeder commerce.

7.3Breaches lead to content removal, trust score penalties, badge revocation, and suspension or removal from the Platform.

8Content Standards

8.1All content you upload (images, text, reviews, listings) must be your own original work or content you have permission to use.

8.2Content must not infringe anyone’s intellectual property, privacy, or other rights.

8.3Reviews must be honest, based on genuine experience, and not part of any incentivised or coordinated scheme.

9Animal Welfare

9.1The Platform is committed to responsible aquatic animal keeping. Users and providers must comply with all applicable animal welfare law.

9.2Any evidence of animal cruelty, neglect, or illegal trading is treated as an L4 (Critical) incident, leading to immediate account action and, where appropriate, referral to the relevant authorities.

10Reporting and Blocking

10.1Report. You can report any content or user from within the Apps, or by emailing support@aquaai.uk.

10.2Block. You can block any user. Their content is removed from your view immediately, they can no longer message you, and we are notified so we can review their content.

10.3Our response. We act on every report of objectionable content within 24 hours: where content breaches this AUP or our Terms, we remove it and remove the user who posted it from the Platform.

11Enforcement

11.1Breaches of this AUP are classified by severity under our Regulatory Framework:

  • L1 (Minor, -5 points): first-time minor infractions, late responses, minor inaccuracies
  • L2 (Moderate, -10 to -15 points): repeated minor infractions, policy breaches, misleading information
  • L3 (Serious, -20 to -25 points): significant policy breaches, data integrity failures, a pattern of poor conduct
  • L4 (Critical, -30 to -40 points): fraud, animal welfare breaches, systemic abuse, illegal activity

11.2Enforcement may include content removal, trust score reduction, badge revocation, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the Platform.

11.3Posting objectionable content or abusing other users (Section 2) and L4 breaches lead to immediate removal from the Platform without prior warning and, where appropriate, referral to the relevant authorities.

12Changes

12.1We may update this AUP from time to time. Changes will be communicated through the Platform.
