Marketplace Policy

Last Updated: May 2026
Classification: Public

1. Introduction

1.1. This Marketplace Policy governs the peer-to-peer marketplace within the AQUA AI® platform. It applies to all users who browse, buy, sell, or bid on marketplace listings.

1.2. The AQUA AI® marketplace is a platform for connecting buyers and sellers of aquatic goods. AQUA AI® does not sell goods directly and does not process payments for marketplace transactions.

2. Eligibility

2.1. All registered users may browse the marketplace and make purchases (including Free tier).

2.2. Only Premium and Pro subscribers may create marketplace listings.

2.3. Breeder providers may list species stock directly from their managed inventory.

3. Listing Standards

3.1. All listings must include:
  • Accurate title and description
  • Accurate pricing in British Pounds Sterling (£)
  • Truthful condition information
  • Clear photographs (original, not stock images)
  • Correct category selection
3.2. Listings must not contain:
  • False, misleading, or exaggerated descriptions
  • Someone else's images without permission
  • Contact information intended to facilitate off-platform transactions
  • Any content that violates the Acceptable Use Policy

4. Listing Types

4.1. Fixed Price

Sellers set a fixed price. Buyers submit a purchase request by tapping "Buy Now." The seller receives a notification and may accept, reject, or request more information. Once accepted, the transaction proceeds directly between buyer and seller.

4.2. Bidding (Auction)

Sellers set a base price. Buyers submit bids at or above the base price. Multiple bids are permitted. The seller selects the winning bid. The transaction proceeds directly between buyer and seller.

5. Permitted Categories

  • Fish: live aquatic fish species (must comply with UK law)
  • Plants: aquatic plants and pond plants
  • Equipment: filters, heaters, lights, pumps, tanks, and related hardware
  • Accessories: decorations, substrates, water treatments, and aquascaping materials

6. Prohibited Items

  • Protected, endangered, or CITES-listed species without appropriate documentation
  • Illegally caught, imported, or smuggled specimens
  • Diseased, injured, or distressed animals
  • Controlled substances or medications
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods
  • Weapons, explosives, or hazardous materials
  • Any item prohibited by UK law

7. Location and Radius

7.1. Listings are visible to users within a 200km radius of the seller's location.

7.2. Location permission is required to create or browse marketplace listings.

7.3. Sellers must not misrepresent their location.

8. Communication

8.1. Buyers and sellers may communicate through the marketplace chat feature attached to each listing.

8.2. Communications must be professional and relevant to the transaction.

8.3. Sharing external contact information to circumvent platform oversight is prohibited.

9. Transaction Responsibility

9.1. AQUA AI® does not process payments for marketplace transactions. All payments are arranged directly between buyer and seller.

9.2. AQUA AI® is not responsible for the quality, condition, legality, or delivery of any item listed on the marketplace.

9.3. Buyers and sellers enter into transactions at their own risk. We strongly recommend meeting in person for livestock transactions and inspecting goods before payment.

10. Trust and Reputation

10.1. Trust scores and badges are displayed on all marketplace profiles. Higher trust scores indicate sustained positive platform activity.

10.2. Marketplace-specific badges include: Trusted Trader, Fast Payer, Reliable Seller, and Fair Dealer.

10.3. Users may leave reviews for marketplace transactions, contributing to the seller's overall trust profile.

11. Enforcement

11.1. Listings that violate this policy will be removed without notice.

11.2. Repeat violations or serious offences (prohibited items, fraud, animal welfare concerns) will trigger trust score penalties and potential account action under the Regulatory Framework.

11.3. Reports of policy violations can be submitted through the in-app reporting function or by emailing info@aquaai.uk.

12. Animal Welfare

12.1. All livestock listings must comply with UK animal welfare legislation.

12.2. Sellers are responsible for ensuring live animals are healthy, humanely housed, and transported in accordance with applicable regulations.

12.3. Any evidence of animal cruelty, neglect, or illegal trading is treated as an L4 (Critical) incident.

Breeder In-App Commerce

In addition to the peer-to-peer marketplace, verified Breeders may sell live livestock and aquatic goods directly through the Platform using in-app purchasing powered by Stripe.

  • Payment ProcessingAll Breeder commerce transactions are processed securely via Stripe. Buyers pay at the point of order. Funds are held in escrow for a mandatory 7-day period.
  • Fulfilment OptionsBuyers may choose to collect in-store (free) or opt for delivery at an additional cost set by the Breeder. The 7-day hold allows sufficient time for either fulfilment method.
  • PayoutUpon expiry of the 7-day hold, funds are released automatically to the Breeder's Stripe Connect account, less the 7% platform commission.
  • Breeder ObligationsBreeders must ensure stock accuracy, provide timely fulfilment, package livestock appropriately for transport, and comply with all applicable animal welfare legislation.
  • Prohibited ConductCircumventing the Stripe payment integration, directing buyers to pay off-platform, or misrepresenting stock availability constitutes a serious policy violation subject to trust score penalties and potential account termination.
  • Distinction from P2PBreeder commerce is a separate transaction flow from the peer-to-peer marketplace. P2P marketplace transactions are arranged directly between users without AQUA AI® payment processing. Breeder commerce transactions are fully processed, held, and settled through the Platform.

Tier-Gated Selling and Community Features

Marketplace Selling

Selling items on the AQUA AI® Marketplace is restricted to users with an active Premium, Pro, or Breeder subscription. Users on the Free tier are restricted to purchasing only.

Community Showcase

Users may list their tanks in the Community Showcase. Transactions or direct sales should be conducted through official Marketplace listings rather than circumventing the platform via Community Showcase messaging. Off-platform transaction attempts detected by our systems may result in trust score penalties and badge revocation.
