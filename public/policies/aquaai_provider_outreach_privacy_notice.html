1Who This Notice Is For

1.1This notice is for aquatic breeders and consultancy businesses we have contacted, or may contact, about joining Aqua Providers. If you have an Aqua AI or Aqua Providers account, our main Privacy Policy applies to your account instead.

1.2AQUAAI LTD is the data controller. Contact privacy@aquaai.uk.

2What We Hold and Where It Comes From

2.1We hold business contact details: business name, contact name and role, business email address, business telephone number, business address, website, and the type of aquatic business.

2.2We obtain these from publicly available sources, including the Companies House register and businesses’ own public websites and directory listings.

2.3We also keep a record of our contact with you: what we sent, when, and whether you replied, opted out, or joined.

3Why We Use It and Our Legal Basis

3.1We use these details to tell your business about Aqua Providers and invite it to join. We rely on our legitimate interest in introducing a relevant business service to aquatic businesses.

3.2We email corporate businesses (such as limited companies) under this legitimate interest. We email individual businesses, such as sole traders and partnerships, only with their prior consent.

3.3Before calling any business telephone number, we screen it against the Telephone Preference Service and the Corporate Telephone Preference Service.

4Sharing

4.1We do not sell these details. They are processed only by the service providers that host our systems and send our email, acting on our instructions. Where any of them processes data outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

5How Long We Keep It

5.1If your business does not join, we delete your details 36 months after our last contact with you.

5.2If you opt out or object, we stop contacting you straight away. We keep only your email address and/or telephone number on a suppression list, indefinitely, so that you are never contacted again.

5.3If your business joins Aqua Providers, our main Privacy Policy applies from then on.

6Your Rights

6.1You can object at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk. You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction. We respond within one month.

6.2You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

7When We Tell You This

7.1Every message we send links to our Privacy Policy, which directs you to this notice.
