1Introduction

1.1This Cookies and Data Policy explains how AQUAAI LTD, trading as Aqua AI, collects, stores, processes, and manages data through the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers mobile applications (the “Apps”) and the website at www.aquaai.uk (the “Website”).

1.2Read it alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions. Where this policy and the Privacy Policy differ, the Privacy Policy applies.

2Data We Collect

2.1Data You Provide

  • Account information (name, email, authentication credentials)
  • Aquarium data (habitats, species, equipment, water parameters, products, dosing records)
  • Images and video for AI analysis (species identification, disease detection, tank scanning)
  • AquaBot queries, including voice queries where you use voice
  • Location data (with your permission, for marketplace, consultant, and breeder discovery)
  • Community Showcase posts, comments, likes, and messages
  • Marketplace listings and listing-chat messages, booking requests, and booking messages
  • Breeder orders, delivery or collection choices, and delivery addresses
  • Reports and blocks you make
  • Provider business data (profiles, listings, stock, availability) and referral data
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe or, for App Store purchases, by Apple. We do not store full card details. Breeders submit identity and bank details directly to Stripe Connect.

2.2Data Collected Automatically

  • Device information (model, OS, unique identifiers, network)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration, navigation paths)
  • Performance and diagnostic data (crash reports, error logs)
  • AI interaction logs (queries, responses, confidence scores)
  • Push notification tokens and delivery status
  • Transaction data (order amounts, payment hold status, commission, payout routing, subscription status)

3How Data Is Stored

3.1On Your Device. Local storage holds your preferences, sign-in tokens, and cached data. On-device data is protected by your device’s own security controls.

3.2In the Cloud. Account data, aquarium data, readings, images you upload, bookings, orders, marketplace listings, community content, and trust score data are stored on Supabase (database and authentication) and AWS S3 (media files). Cloud storage is encrypted at rest and protected by access controls.

3.3AI Models. Our species database (20,000+ species) and AI models run on secure cloud infrastructure. AI requests are sent over encrypted connections.

4How Data Is Processed

4.1AI Processing. Images, video, queries, and readings you submit are sent to our cloud AI services for identification, disease detection, water analysis, compatibility assessment, care plan generation, health scoring, and AquaBot. Species identification and image analysis are provided by Google’s Gemini API; language model processing and content moderation are provided by OpenAI. All processors act under data processing agreements with us.

4.2Content Moderation. Content you post, and content reported by other users, may be checked by automated tools and reviewed by our team to enforce our zero-tolerance policy on objectionable content.

5Analytics and Intelligence

5.1We use anonymised, aggregated data to improve the Platform, including feature usage patterns, AI accuracy metrics, marketplace activity trends, booking completion rates, trust score distribution, and species identification frequency.

5.2This analytics data is processed at Layer 3 (MI/BI) of our architecture and contains no personal data.

5.3We use it to improve AI accuracy and recommendations, optimise the user experience, and provide aggregate insights to the aquatic industry.

6Third-Party Data Sharing

6.1Processors. Stripe (payments and provider payouts), Supabase (database and authentication), AWS (infrastructure and storage), OpenAI (AI processing and moderation), Google (Gemini API, species identification), Perplexity (AI research for provider tools), Resend (email delivery), Google Workspace (email and support), Expo (push notifications), and Geoapify (geolocation). Each processes data only on our instructions under a written agreement.

6.2Independent Third Parties. Apple (App Store purchases and refunds), Stripe (identity checks for provider payout accounts), and Google, Apple, and Facebook (sign-in, if you use it) process data under their own privacy policies.

6.3Product Recommendations. Product recommendations use the Amazon Associates programme. Your interactions with product recommendations are used to improve future recommendations. If you follow a product link, you leave Aqua AI and Amazon’s privacy policy applies.

7Data Retention

7.1We keep your data while your account is active.

7.2When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised straight away. Records needed for financial, tax, and accounting purposes are kept in anonymised form only. Backup copies are removed as backups expire on their normal cycle. See Privacy Policy Section 9 for the full retention rules.

7.3Anonymised analytics are kept indefinitely. They contain no personal data.

7.4Data stored on your device stays under your control and is removed when you delete the app.

8Your Controls

8.1Device Permissions. Control access in your device settings: camera (AI scanning), microphone (AquaBot voice queries, where available), location (marketplace, consultant, and breeder discovery), photo library (image upload), and notifications (alerts and updates). Declining a permission only disables the feature that needs it.

8.2Consents. Withdraw location or voice consent at any time by turning off location or microphone access in your device settings. Withdraw marketing consent through the unsubscribe link in any message or by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk.

8.3Access and Deletion. Request a copy of your data, or correction of it, by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk. We respond within one month. Delete your account at any time in the Apps (Profile/Settings → Delete account) or at www.aquaai.uk/profile/delete/.

8.4AI Improvement Opt-Out. You can ask for your data to be excluded from AI model improvement by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk. This does not affect the AI features available to you.

9Cookies and Similar Technologies

9.1The Apps do not use browser cookies.

9.2The Apps use local storage (preferences, sign-in tokens, cached data), device identifiers (analytics, push notifications, security), and short-lived authentication tokens.

9.3The Website uses only cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work, such as keeping you signed in. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies or scripts. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent before any are set.

10Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are communicated through the Apps or Website, and the “Last Updated” date shows the latest revision.
