1. Introduction

1.1. This Cookies and Data Policy explains how Aqua AI (operated by Humara Ltd) collects, uses, stores, and manages data through the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers mobile applications (the "Apps") and the website at www.aquaai.uk (the "Website").

1.2. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

2. Data We Collect

2.1. User-Provided Data
  • Account information (email, name, authentication credentials)
  • Aquarium setup data (tanks, species, equipment, water parameters)
  • Images and video for AI analysis (species identification, disease detection, tank scanning)
  • Location data (with permission, for marketplace and consultant discovery)
  • Marketplace listings, booking requests, and communications
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe; we do not store full card details)
  • Breeder in-app order data, transaction metadata (order amounts, 7-day hold status, payout routing), and financial identity data submitted to Stripe Connect by Breeders
2.2. Automatically Collected Data
  • Device information (model, OS, unique identifiers, network)
  • App usage data (features used, session duration, navigation paths)
  • Performance and diagnostic data (crash reports, error logs)
  • AI interaction logs (queries, responses, confidence scores)
  • Push notification tokens and delivery status

3. How Data Is Stored

3.1. On-Device Storage

Images captured for AI identification are processed locally on your device where possible. Local storage is used for user preferences, cached data, and performance optimisation. On-device data is subject to your device's own security controls.

3.2. Cloud Storage

Account data, tank configurations, water readings, booking records, marketplace listings, and trust score data are stored on our cloud infrastructure: Supabase (primary database and authentication), AWS S3 (media file storage). All cloud storage is encrypted at rest and protected by access controls.

3.3. AI Model Data

Our species database (20,000+ species) and AI models are hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. AI inference requests are processed through encrypted API connections.

4. Data Processing

4.1. On-Device Processing

Species and equipment identification may occur on-device using downloaded models. Water parameter analysis from test strip photos is processed on-device where supported. This reduces data transmission and improves response times.

4.2. Cloud Processing

Complex AI analysis (disease detection, compatibility assessment, care plan generation, health scoring) is processed via cloud APIs. AI chat interactions are processed through OpenAI's language models. All cloud processing is subject to our data processing agreements with providers.

5. Analytics and Intelligence

5.1. We collect anonymised and aggregated data to improve the Platform, including: feature usage patterns, AI accuracy metrics, marketplace activity trends, booking completion rates, trust score distribution, and species identification frequency.

5.2. This analytics data is processed at Layer 3 (MI/BI Intelligence) of our architecture and does not contain personally identifiable information.

5.3. Analytics data helps us improve AI model accuracy, enhance product recommendations, optimise the user experience, and provide aggregate insights to the aquatic industry.

6. Third-Party Data Sharing

6.1. Service Providers

We share data with service providers who assist in Platform operation: Stripe (payment processing and Stripe Connect payouts), Supabase (data hosting), AWS (cloud infrastructure), OpenAI (AI processing), Expo (push notifications), and Geoapify (geolocation services). All providers are contractually obligated to process data only on our instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

6.2. Product Recommendations

Product recommendations use Amazon Affiliate integration. When you interact with product recommendations, your interaction data is used to improve future recommendations. If you click through to Amazon, you leave the Aqua AI platform and are subject to Amazon's own privacy policy.

7. Data Retention

Data Type Retention Period
Account and profile data Duration of active account plus 30 days
Tank and species data Duration of active account
AI interaction logs 24 months, then anonymised
Booking records 36 months
Trust score and badge data Account lifetime plus 12 months

8. Your Controls

8.1. Device Permissions

You can control the Apps' access to device features through your device settings:

  • Camera (required for AI scanning)
  • Microphone (for video analysis)
  • Location services (for marketplace and consultant discovery)
  • Photo library (for image upload)
  • Notifications (for alerts and updates)
8.2. Data Access and Deletion

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account and associated data by contacting info@aquaai.uk or through the in-app settings. We will process requests within 30 days.

8.3. AI Processing Opt-Out

You may request that your data is excluded from AI model improvement processes by contacting us. This will not affect the AI services available to you.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

9.1. The Aqua AI mobile applications do not use traditional browser cookies.

9.2. We use the following device-level technologies:

  • Local storage: for preferences, authentication tokens, and cached data
  • Device identifiers: for analytics, push notifications, and security
  • Session tokens: JWT-based authentication tokens with short-lived access and refresh rotation

9.3. The Website (www.aquaai.uk) may use standard cookies for functionality, analytics, and preference storage. Cookie consent is obtained through our website cookie banner.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be communicated through the Apps or Website. The "Last Updated" date indicates the most recent revision.
