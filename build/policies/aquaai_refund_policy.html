Refund Policy

When subscriptions, bookings and purchases can be refunded.

1Introduction

1.1This Refund Policy explains how refunds work across the Aqua AI platform. It covers subscriptions, consultant bookings, breeder commerce, and peer-to-peer marketplace transactions.

1.2Refunds for payments we process are made through Stripe to the original payment method. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple (Section 2.5).

1.3Referral platform credit is never refunded as cash. Where a refunded purchase was paid wholly or partly with credit, the credit portion is returned to your credit balance.

2Subscription Refunds

2.1Monthly Subscriptions. Monthly charges are non-refundable once the billing period has started. If you cancel, you keep your tier’s features until the end of the current billing period.

2.2Annual Subscriptions. Annual subscriptions are non-refundable once the billing period has started, except where consumer protection law requires otherwise. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the annual period.

2.3Cooling-Off Period (subscriptions purchased from Aqua AI). Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, you have a 14-day cooling-off period from the date of purchase. If you cancel within this period and have not substantively used the paid features, you are entitled to a full refund. Contact support@aquaai.uk within 14 days of purchase.

2.4Tier Downgrades. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds are given for the remaining period.

2.5App Store Purchases. Subscriptions bought through Apple In-App Purchase are billed by Apple. Aqua AI cannot issue refunds for them. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Cancelling or deleting your Aqua AI account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

3Consultant Booking Refunds

3.1How Bookings Are Paid. Aqua AI’s booking fee is 10% of the service price and is paid in the app to confirm the booking, by card, with platform credit, or both. Credit can only be used for the booking fee. The remaining 90% is paid directly to the consultant and is outside this policy.

3.2Cancellation by the Consultant. The booking fee is refunded, to your card or as credit if you paid with credit. Aqua AI absorbs the Stripe processing fee. Card refunds usually arrive within 5–10 business days. Instead of cancelling, the consultant may offer an alternative time, which applies only if you agree.

3.3Cancellation by the User. The booking fee is not refunded, whether paid by card or with credit. Users are encouraged to reschedule; the reschedule option is shown before a cancellation is confirmed.

3.4No-Show. If you do not attend without cancelling, the booking fee is not refunded. If the consultant does not attend, you are refunded as in 3.2, and the incident is recorded against the consultant’s trust score.

3.5Trust Scores. Cancelling with 48 hours’ notice or more carries no trust score penalty. Cancelling with less than 48 hours’ notice is subject to trust score accountability (L1–L4 escalation based on pattern).

3.6Rescheduling. Rescheduling does not involve any payment. It only updates the booking record. No refunds or extra charges apply.

4Breeder Commerce Refunds

4.1Cancellation by the Breeder. If a breeder cancels an order before fulfilment, a full refund is issued automatically to the buyer’s original payment method via Stripe (any portion paid with platform credit is returned as credit).

4.2Cancellation by the Buyer. Buyers may cancel before collection or dispatch. If the breeder has not yet prepared the order, a full refund is issued. If the breeder has already prepared it, cancellation is at the breeder’s discretion.

4.3Non-Collection. If a buyer chooses in-store collection and does not collect within 7 days of the order being ready, the breeder may mark the order as not collected. The buyer is refunded in full automatically to the original payment method (any portion paid with platform credit is returned as credit). No fee is charged. The missed collection is recorded against the buyer’s trust score.

4.4After Release. Funds are released to the breeder when a collection order is collected, or when the 7-day hold on a delivery order ends. Once funds have been released, Aqua AI cannot issue a refund directly. Post-fulfilment disputes, including Dead on Arrival claims, must be raised with the breeder.

4.5Breeders sell to you as traders. Nothing in this section affects your statutory rights.

5Peer-to-Peer Marketplace

5.1Aqua AI does not process payments for peer-to-peer marketplace transactions and does not issue refunds for them. Disputes are between the buyer and seller.

5.2Patterns of failed transactions, dishonesty, or poor conduct affect trust scores and may lead to marketplace restrictions.

6Exceptional Circumstances

6.1Where there is a platform error, billing error, or service failure caused by Aqua AI, we will review refund requests case by case.

6.2Send these requests to support@aquaai.uk with a description of the issue and any evidence.

6.3Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights as a consumer.

7How to Request a Refund

  • Subscriptions purchased from Aqua AI (cooling-off period): email support@aquaai.uk within 14 days of purchase.
  • App Store subscriptions: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Consultant cancellations and breeder cancellations before release: processed through the app.
  • Everything else: support@aquaai.uk.
