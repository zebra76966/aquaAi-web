Exceptions

Breeder Commerce Refunds
Pre-Fulfilment Cancellation

If a Breeder cancels an order before the 7-day hold expires and before fulfilment, a full refund is automatically issued to the buyer's original payment method via Stripe.

Post-Release Disputes

Once the 7-day hold has expired and funds have been released to the Breeder, Aqua AI cannot issue a direct refund. Buyers must raise post-fulfilment disputes (including Dead on Arrival claims) directly with the Breeder.

Buyer Cancellation

Buyers may cancel an order before collection or dispatch. If the Breeder has not yet prepared the order for fulfilment, a full refund is issued. If the Breeder has already prepared the order, the cancellation is at the Breeder's discretion.

Non-Collection

If a buyer selects in-store collection and fails to collect within the 7-day hold period, the order may be cancelled by the Breeder and funds returned to the buyer, less any reasonable restocking fee as disclosed in the listing.

Platform/billing errors reviewed case-by-case. Email info@aquaai.uk.

Promotional Pricing Grace Period

If you cancel a grandfathered promotional subscription, a 30-day grace period applies. Resubscribing within 30 days restores your promotional rate. After 30 days, standard pricing applies and the promotional entitlement is permanently forfeited.
