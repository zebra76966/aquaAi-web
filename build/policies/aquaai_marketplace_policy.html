1Introduction

1.1This Marketplace Policy governs buying and selling on the Aqua AI platform. It covers two separate transaction flows:

  • Peer-to-peer marketplace: users buy and sell aquatic goods directly with each other. Aqua AI connects buyers and sellers but does not sell goods or process payments.
  • Breeder commerce: verified breeders sell livestock and aquatic goods in the app. Aqua AI processes, holds, and settles these payments through Stripe (Section 13).

1.2This policy forms part of our Terms and Conditions. You must be 18 or over to use the marketplace.

2Eligibility

2.1All registered users, including the Free tier, may browse and buy.

2.2Only users with an active Premium or Pro subscription may create peer-to-peer listings.

2.3Breeder providers may list species stock directly from their managed inventory.

3Listing Standards

3.1Every listing must include an accurate title and description, accurate pricing in British Pounds Sterling (£), truthful condition information, clear original photographs (not stock images), and the correct category.

3.2Listings must not contain false, misleading, or exaggerated descriptions; other people’s images without permission; contact details intended to move the transaction off-platform; objectionable content; or anything else that breaches the Acceptable Use Policy.

4Listing Types

4.1Fixed Price. The seller sets a price. Buyers tap “Buy Now” to send a purchase request. The seller can accept, reject, or ask for more information. Once accepted, the transaction is completed directly between buyer and seller.

4.2Bidding (Auction). The seller sets a base price. Buyers bid at or above it, and multiple bids are allowed. The seller chooses the winning bid, and the transaction is completed directly between buyer and seller.

5Permitted Categories

  • Fish: live aquatic fish species (must comply with UK law)
  • Plants: aquatic and pond plants
  • Equipment: filters, heaters, lights, pumps, tanks, and related hardware
  • Accessories: decorations, substrates, water treatments, and aquascaping materials

6Prohibited Items

  • Protected, endangered, or CITES-listed species without the required documentation
  • Illegally caught, imported, or smuggled specimens
  • Diseased, injured, or distressed animals
  • Controlled substances or medications
  • Counterfeit or stolen goods
  • Weapons, explosives, or hazardous materials
  • Any item prohibited by UK law

7Location and Radius

7.1Listings are visible to users within 200 km of the seller’s location.

7.2Location permission is required to create or browse listings.

7.3Sellers must not misrepresent their location.

8Communication

8.1Buyers and sellers communicate through the chat attached to each listing. Aqua AI does not share either party’s email address.

8.2Communications must be professional and relevant to the transaction. The zero-tolerance standards in the Acceptable Use Policy apply to all marketplace messages.

8.3Sharing external contact details to get around platform oversight is prohibited.

8.4You can report a listing or message, or block a user, at any time. We act on reports within 24 hours, removing breaching content and the user who posted it.

9Transaction Responsibility (Peer-to-Peer)

9.1Aqua AI does not process payments for peer-to-peer transactions. Buyers and sellers arrange payment directly.

9.2Aqua AI is not responsible for the quality, condition, legality, or delivery of any peer-to-peer item.

9.3Buyers and sellers transact at their own risk. For livestock, we strongly recommend meeting in person and inspecting goods before paying.

9.4Peer-to-peer sellers are usually private individuals, not traders. Consumer rights that apply when you buy from a business may not apply to a purchase from a private seller.

9.5Breeders sell to you as traders. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights when buying from a breeder.

10Trust and Reputation

10.1Trust scores and badges are shown on all marketplace profiles. Higher scores reflect sustained positive platform activity.

10.2Marketplace badges include Trusted Trader, Fast Payer, Reliable Seller, and Fair Dealer.

10.3Users may leave reviews for marketplace transactions, which contribute to the seller’s trust profile.

11Enforcement

11.1Listings that breach this policy are removed without notice.

11.2Repeat or serious breaches (prohibited items, fraud, animal welfare concerns) lead to trust score penalties and account action under the Regulatory Framework, up to removal from the Platform. Objectionable content and abuse of other users lead to immediate removal from the Platform under our zero-tolerance policy.

11.3Report breaches in the app or by emailing support@aquaai.uk.

12Animal Welfare

12.1All livestock listings must comply with UK animal welfare law.

12.2Sellers must ensure live animals are healthy, humanely housed, and transported in line with applicable regulations.

12.3Any evidence of animal cruelty, neglect, or illegal trading is treated as an L4 (Critical) incident.

13Breeder Commerce

13.1Payment. All breeder commerce transactions are processed through Stripe. Buyers pay when they order. Funds for delivery orders are held for 7 days. Funds for collection orders are held until the buyer collects.

13.2Fulfilment. Buyers choose in-store collection (free) or delivery at a cost set by the breeder. Delivery is available only from breeders whose local council licence for selling animals as pets has been approved by Aqua AI and is in date; if a licence expires or an approval is withdrawn, delivery pauses until a current licence is approved, and collection is unaffected. For collection, the buyer receives a collection code when the order is ready and collects within 7 days; the breeder enters the code to confirm the handover. If the buyer does not collect within 7 days, the breeder may mark the order as not collected: the buyer is refunded in full, the breeder’s stock is restored, and the missed collection is recorded against the buyer’s trust score, not the breeder’s.

13.3Payout. Funds are released automatically to the breeder’s Stripe Connect account, less the 7% platform commission: for collection orders, as soon as the collection code is entered; for delivery orders, when the 7-day hold ends.

13.4Platform credit. Buyers may use referral platform credit towards any breeder purchase, with no cap. The breeder still receives their full payout, calculated on the full purchase value. If a credit-funded purchase is refunded, the credit portion is returned as credit, not cash.

13.5Breeder obligations. Breeders must keep stock accurate, fulfil orders on time, package livestock properly for transport, and comply with all applicable animal welfare law.

13.6Prohibited conduct. Getting around the Stripe payment integration, directing buyers to pay off-platform, or misrepresenting stock availability is a serious breach, subject to trust score penalties and possible account termination.

14Community Showcase

14.1Users may display their tanks in the Community Showcase. Sales must go through marketplace listings or breeder commerce, not Showcase messaging. Attempts to move transactions off-platform may lead to trust score penalties and badge revocation.
