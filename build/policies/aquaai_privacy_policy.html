1Introduction

1.1This Privacy Policy explains how AQUAAI LTD, trading as Aqua AI (Company Number 16455928), collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers mobile applications and the website at www.aquaai.uk.

1.2We comply with the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003.

1.3AQUAAI LTD is the data controller. Our data protection contact is reachable at privacy@aquaai.uk.

2Information We Collect

2.1Information You Provide

  • Account information: name, email address, password, and authentication data (Google, Apple, Facebook)
  • Profile information: display name, profile image, bio, address, contact preferences, and profile visibility settings
  • Aquarium data: habitat configurations (name, type, volume, water type), species, equipment details (type, brand, wattage, model number), water parameters (standard and saltwater), care schedules, and maintenance logs
  • Product data: tank products, dosing records (dosage amounts, schedules, completion history), and reorder preferences
  • Images and video: photographs and recordings for species identification, disease detection, tank scanning, Community Showcase posts, and marketplace listings
  • AquaBot queries, including voice queries where you use voice
  • Community content: Community Showcase posts, images, descriptions, comments, likes, and messages with other users
  • Marketplace content: listing descriptions, images, pricing, categories, and location
  • Booking information: service requirements, scheduling, consultation descriptions, and booking messages
  • Breeder purchases: order details, delivery or collection preferences, delivery address, and follow-up communications
  • Provider data: business profiles, service listings, stock levels, pricing, availability schedules, health/mortality data (entered manually), business AI assistant interactions, and the breeder licences and qualification certificates you upload (document image, licence or certificate number, issuer, and issue and expiry dates), with the result of our check against the issuer’s public register. Providers submit identity and bank details directly to Stripe Connect for payouts; Aqua AI does not store them.
  • Reports and blocks: content or users you report, the reason you give, and the users you block
  • Referral data: your referral code, the accounts you referred or were referred by, and the credit earned

2.2Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: model, OS, unique identifiers, mobile network, browser type
  • Usage data: features used, session duration, interactions, navigation paths, AquaBot query frequency
  • Performance data: crash reports, error logs, diagnostics
  • Location data: approximate and precise (with your permission) for the marketplace, consultants, and breeders
  • AI interaction data: queries, responses, confidence scores, care plan generation events, health score calculations
  • Transaction data: order amounts, payment hold status, commission calculations, payout routing, subscription status and source (Website or App Store)

2.3Information From Third Parties

  • Apple: when you subscribe through the App Store, Apple sends us transaction information (product, purchase and expiry dates, renewal and refund status) so we can apply your subscription. Apple does not share your payment card details with us.
  • Sign-in providers: if you sign in with Google, Apple, or Facebook, we receive your name and email address from that provider.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI services: species identification, disease detection, water analysis, AI Water Intelligence, compatibility assessment, care plans, product recommendations with dosage calculations, health scoring, preventative alerts, comparative species tracking, and AquaBot
  • To run consultant bookings, including sharing pre-diagnosis data with booked consultants
  • To run the breeder directory and breeder purchases, including sharing order and delivery details with the breeder fulfilling your order
  • To run the marketplace and its purchase request flow
  • To run the Community Showcase and messaging
  • To moderate content: automated filtering, automated and human review of reports, acting on blocks, and removing content and users that breach our Terms
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions (Stripe and Apple)
  • To calculate and maintain trust scores, badges, incident records, and referral credit
  • To power provider tools: Analytics, Trust Intelligence, Research, and the Provider AI Business Assistant
  • To improve AI accuracy through aggregated, anonymised analysis
  • To comply with legal obligations

4Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: providing the services you signed up or subscribed to, including bookings, purchases, and subscriptions
  • Legitimate interests: improving the Platform, preventing fraud, content moderation and user safety, trust scoring, and aggregated analytics
  • Consent: location data, voice processing, and marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time: location and microphone access in your device settings, and marketing through the unsubscribe link in any message or by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk.
  • Legal obligation: keeping financial records and responding to lawful requests

5Intelligence Layer and AI Processing

5.1Aqua AI operates a three-layer intelligence architecture:

  • Layer 1 (Operational): raw inputs such as tank parameters, species, readings, and bookings. Never overwritten by AI.
  • Layer 2 (Analytical): AI-generated insights, recommendations, dosage calculations, and health scores. Probabilistic, logged, and auditable.
  • Layer 3 (MI/BI): aggregated, anonymised analytics for platform improvement. Contains no personal data.

5.2Provider AI tools (Business Assistant, Research, Trust Intelligence) use your provider data to generate personalised business insights based on your activity, listings, and performance.

5.3Automated trust scoring. Trust scores and badges are calculated automatically from your platform activity. They affect your ranking, visibility in search and the marketplace, and can lead to feature restrictions. You can ask for a person to review any automated trust score decision that affects you by emailing support@aquaai.uk.

6Businesses Contacted About Aqua Providers

6.1If we contacted your business about joining Aqua Providers, our Provider Outreach Privacy Notice at www.aquaai.uk/provider-outreach-privacy explains how we handle your business contact details.

7Data Sharing

7.1We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as follows:

  • Consultants: pre-diagnosis data for booked services. Sharing stops on completion or cancellation.
  • Breeders: order details, delivery address, and contact information needed to fulfil your purchase.
  • Marketplace counterparties: your display name and the messages you exchange in the listing chat. We do not share your email address with them.
  • Other users: your public profile, trust score, badges, marketplace listings, and any Community Showcase content you choose to make visible.
  • Your referrer: if someone referred you, they see your display username and the reward value only, never your plan or billing details.
  • Service providers (processors) acting on our instructions: Stripe (payment processing and provider payouts), Supabase (database and authentication), Amazon Web Services (infrastructure and storage), OpenAI (AI processing), Google (Gemini API, species identification and image analysis), Perplexity (AI research for provider tools), Resend (transactional email), Google Workspace (email and support), Expo (push notifications), and Geoapify (geolocation).
  • Independent controllers: Apple (App Store purchases and refunds); Stripe (identity verification and fraud prevention for provider payout accounts); Google, Apple, and Facebook (sign-in, if you use it); and Amazon (product links you choose to follow). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and business transfers: where required by law, or as part of a business sale or reorganisation.

8Data Storage and Security

8.1Our primary database is Supabase (PostgreSQL). Files are stored in AWS S3. AI processing uses secure API connections.

8.2We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit and at rest, authenticated access, and hashed password storage.

9Data Retention and Account Deletion

9.1We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active.

9.2You can delete your account at any time in the Apps (Profile/Settings → Delete account) or at www.aquaai.uk/profile/delete/. When you do, your personal data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised straight away, so it can no longer be linked to you. Copies held in our secure backups are removed as those backups expire on their normal rotation cycle.

9.3Records we must keep for financial, tax, and accounting purposes (orders, bookings, payments, payouts, and subscription history) are kept in anonymised form only.

9.4Stripe and Apple keep their own records of transactions they process, under their own legal obligations and privacy policies.

9.5We keep a record that a deletion took place. This record contains no personal data.

9.6If we remove you from the Platform for breaching our Terms, we keep a one-way hashed identifier and the moderation record so that you cannot re-register. We do this under our legitimate interest in keeping users safe.

9.7Anonymised analytics are kept indefinitely. They contain no personal data.

10Your Rights (UK GDPR)

10.1You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent.

10.2Contact privacy@aquaai.uk. We respond within one month, which may be extended by up to two further months for complex requests; we will tell you if so.

10.3You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

11International Transfers

11.1Some of our service providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US Data Bridge where the recipient is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

12Age Restriction

12.1The Platform is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will close it and delete the data.

13Changes

13.1We will notify you of material changes through the Apps or by email.
