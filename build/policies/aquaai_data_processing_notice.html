1Purpose and Status

1.1AQUAAI LTD, trading as Aqua AI (“Aqua AI”, “we”), is the data controller for personal data processed on the Aqua AI and Aqua Providers apps and the website at www.aquaai.uk (the “Platform”).

1.2This Notice explains how we govern the third-party service providers (“processors”) that process personal data on our behalf, and the safeguards we apply. It is provided for transparency. It is not an agreement you are asked to sign. Your rights as a user are set out in our Privacy Policy.

1.3Each processor is bound by a written data processing agreement with Aqua AI, as required by Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

1.4This Notice is governed by the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2Definitions

2.1“Personal Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (UK GDPR Article 4(1)).

2.2“Processing” means any operation performed on personal data (UK GDPR Article 4(2)).

2.3“Processor” means a third party that processes personal data on Aqua AI’s behalf and on our documented instructions.

3Our Obligations as Controller

3.1As controller, Aqua AI is responsible for deciding the purposes and means of processing; making sure every processing activity has a lawful basis; keeping records of processing activities (UK GDPR Article 30); applying appropriate technical and organisational measures; and notifying the ICO and affected individuals of personal data breaches where required.

4Categories of Data Processed

  • Identity data: name, email address, profile information
  • Authentication data: login credentials and third-party sign-in tokens
  • Aquarium data: tank configurations, species, water parameters, equipment, care schedules
  • AI interaction data: text and voice queries, images, video, AI-generated insights
  • Transactional data: booking records, payment references, subscription status and source (Website or App Store), breeder orders, payout records
  • Marketplace data: listings, bids, purchase requests, seller profiles
  • Community data: Community Showcase posts, comments, likes, and messages
  • Moderation data: reports, blocks, and moderation decisions
  • Trust data: trust scores, badge history, incident records
  • Communication data: booking, marketplace, and Showcase messages
  • Device and usage data: device identifiers, app usage patterns, performance metrics
  • Location data: approximate and precise location (with consent)

5Processors

5.1Aqua AI uses the following processors:

  • Supabase Inc.: database hosting, authentication, real-time data services
  • Amazon Web Services: cloud computing and object storage (S3)
  • Stripe: payment processing, subscription billing, and breeder and consultant payouts through Stripe Connect, including transaction amounts, commission calculations, and payment holds on delivery orders.
  • OpenAI: language model processing for AI features and content moderation
  • Google (Gemini API): species identification and image analysis
  • Perplexity: AI research processing for provider Research and Business Assistant tools
  • Resend: transactional email delivery
  • Google (Workspace): business email and support communications
  • Expo: push notification delivery
  • Geoapify GmbH: geolocation services

5.2These third parties process data as independent controllers under their own terms, not as our processors:

  • Apple: App Store purchases, billing, and refunds
  • Google, Apple, and Facebook: sign-in, where you choose to use them
  • Amazon (Associates programme): product links you choose to follow
  • Stripe: identity verification and fraud prevention for provider payout accounts. Providers submit their legal name, address, date of birth, and bank details directly to Stripe; Aqua AI does not store them.

5.3Each processor may only process data on our documented instructions, must apply appropriate security measures, and must help us respond to data subject rights requests.

5.4We will notify users of material changes to this list through the Platform or by email.

6Security Measures

6.1Aqua AI applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • authenticated access
  • hashed password storage

7International Transfers

7.1Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US Data Bridge where the recipient is certified) or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, in line with Chapter V of the UK GDPR.

8Data Subject Rights

8.1We support the rights in Chapter III of the UK GDPR. Processors are contractually required to help. We respond to requests within one month. Contact privacy@aquaai.uk.

9Data Breach Notification

9.1If a personal data breach occurs, Aqua AI will assess it within 24 hours of discovery; notify the ICO within 72 hours where it is likely to result in a risk to individuals’ rights; notify affected individuals without undue delay where it is likely to result in a high risk; and record the breach, its effects, and the remedial action taken.

10Retention and Deletion

10.1Personal data is kept only as long as needed, as set out in our Privacy Policy. When an account is deleted, personal data in our systems is deleted or irreversibly anonymised straight away. Copies held in backups, and by processors on our behalf, are removed on their normal retention cycles. Financial records are kept in anonymised form only.

11Audit

11.1Aqua AI may audit its processors’ compliance. You can ask about our processing by emailing privacy@aquaai.uk.
